1. Overview & Qualifications
The Gold Tier Health Plan requires applicants to be between 18 and 65 years old. The base monthly premium is $450.

2. Renewal & Grace Period
Policies expire annually. A 30-day grace period is granted for late payments. If payment is not received within 30 days, the policy lapses. Renewal forms must be submitted 15 days before expiration.

3. Out of Scope & Escalation
Complex claims involving pre-existing rare medical conditions must be escalated to a human supervisor immediately.
